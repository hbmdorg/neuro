# DBS and VNS Battery Exchanges
DBS and VNS devices both have pulse generators implanted under the clavicle, similar to a pacemaker.
Battery exchanges are short, simple procedures, often done under GA with an LMA.
There is no neuromonitoring, no pinning, etc., so these do not require any specialized anesthesia.
As always, use your best clinical judgement. 

## A few helpful tips: 
* A device rep should be present to turn the device on/off and reprogram it as necessary.  
* Patients with Parkinson’s Disease should take their PD meds as close to their scheduled time as possible and patients with Epilepsy should take their anti-seizure meds before surgery. 
* The surgeons may turn the patient’s head; make sure your LMA sits well.  
* The surgeons often use local anesthetic so opioids may be used sparingly. This will also help facilitate a postop neuro exam.
