# Seizure & Airway Crisis Simulator
### Awake craniotomy — intraoperative seizure and airway rescue

An interactive, browser-based crisis simulator for teaching recognition and management of a stimulation-induced seizure during awake craniotomy.

**Requires a desktop or laptop browser** — the monitor layout and facilitator controls need a landscape screen and don't work on a phone or narrow window.
