Seizure during Awake Craniotomy — Crisis Simulator

Simulator courtesy of Soowon Lee, MD, PhD — Department of Anesthesiology and Pain Medicine, Seoul National University Bundang Hospital.

You are the anesthesiologist for an awake craniotomy. The patient is awake and cooperating with language mapping. Watch the monitor: vital signs and the ECoG/EEG are live. Read each situation, then choose the action your team would take — the patient responds to what you pick.

Three phases: ① recognize the seizure → ② terminate it (non-pharmacological + pharmacological) → ③ rescue the airway as SpO₂ and ORi fall. A facilitator can also drive events from the ⚙ panel (press F).

Presenter view opens a second window — drag it to the projector and make it full-screen. It shows only the simulator; this window keeps your script and controls.
